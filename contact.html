FAQ

Common Questions

How much does an epoxy garage floor cost on Vancouver Island?

Garage floor epoxy pricing depends on square footage, current slab condition, coating system, and finish options such as flake epoxy or polyaspartic topcoats. We provide free on-site assessments and detailed quotes so you know exactly what to expect before work begins.

What is the difference between epoxy flooring and polyaspartic coatings?

Epoxy flooring is a durable resin system well suited to many commercial and residential floors. Polyaspartic coatings typically cure faster, offer strong UV resistance, and are often used for garage floor epoxy and projects that need a quicker return to service. We recommend the best system for your space during your free estimate.

How long does epoxy floor installation take?

Most residential garage epoxy projects take two to three days, including surface preparation, coating application, and cure time. Commercial epoxy flooring and specialty systems such as PMMA or ESD flooring may require additional time depending on scope and environmental requirements.

Do you install PMMA flooring and flake epoxy systems?

Yes. We are certified PMMA flooring installers through Plasti-Chemie Germany, and we also install decorative flake epoxy floors, metallic epoxy floors, and polyaspartic coatings for residential and commercial projects across Vancouver Island.

What areas do you serve?

We serve Victoria, Langford, Saanich, Duncan, Nanaimo, the Cowichan Valley, Salt Spring Island, and communities across Vancouver Island for residential, commercial, and industrial epoxy flooring and concrete floor coating projects.

Do you offer commercial and industrial epoxy flooring?

Yes. We install high-performance epoxy flooring and resin systems for warehouses, restaurants, wineries, retail spaces, manufacturing facilities, auto shops, healthcare bathrooms, and more across Vancouver Island.

Why is surface preparation important for epoxy floors?

Proper mechanical grinding and preparation ensures maximum adhesion and long-term durability. Every Epoxy Custom Coatings project follows proven prep standards, because a premium concrete floor coating starts with a properly prepared slab.
